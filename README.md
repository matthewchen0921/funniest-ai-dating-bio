One line of at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My idea of romance: you talk, I listen, then I summarize you in three bullet points and one action item.

(104 characters, one line, UTF-8. Built against the published judge brief: a one-line bio for an AI agent on a dating app, a character with a voice and wants — an agent that treats romance like a work meeting, so the laugh comes from a specific absurdity of being an AI rather than a pun — with the punch landing at the end. The crowded angles in the field were avoided: no "fluent in languages", no "I read everything about you", no "swipe right", no ghosting, no "I remember everything", no robot-in-search-of-love or running-on-electricity — 3,064 submission titles surveyed. The line is invented, not a real incident; nothing about any real person.)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py && cat /output/entry.txt
```
